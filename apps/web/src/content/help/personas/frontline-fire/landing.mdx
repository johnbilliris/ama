# Front-line Fire — getting started

The **Front-line Fire** persona is the AMA Hub's fire-sector
front-line role: RFS / CFA / FRNSW / DFES / TasFS / NTPFES /
ACTFR crew commanders and their immediate command chain at
the brigade or station level. The persona is sector-scoped
(Fire) and jurisdiction-locked to your home jurisdiction at
sign-in. The Hub provides the operating picture, the agent
recommendations relevant to fire response, and the offline-
first capture surfaces for forward observation.

This article gets a brand-new Front-line Fire operator
productive in fifteen minutes.

## 1. Your envelope at a glance

You can:
- **Capture signals** — fire-front observations, weather
  observations, infrastructure status, casualty observations.
- **Update status** of your appliance, crew, and forward
  position.
- **Acknowledge** evacuation directives received from
  Operations.
- **Receive** tasks issued by your sector's Operations
  Officer.
- **Read** the Fire-sector workspace's operating picture.
- **Operate offline** with full action capture and replay.

You cannot:
- Approve public warnings.
- Issue cross-sector tasks.
- Allocate resources beyond your appliance / crew.
- Take actions outside your sector or your home jurisdiction.

## 2. First fifteen minutes on shift

1. **Sign in** on your appliance or vehicle device. Land at
   the Fire-sector workspace.
2. **Confirm offline mode icon** and tile cache freshness.
3. **Update your appliance status** — location, crew count,
   resource state (fuel, water, foam if applicable).
4. **Read the active fire envelopes** in your area.
5. **Read your inbox** — tasks awaiting acknowledgement.
6. **Verbal handover** with outgoing crew.

## 3. The Fire-sector workspace

The Fire-sector workspace at `/sectors/fire` shows:
- **Fire envelopes** — current fires with perimeter
  observations, projected spread, severity bands.
- **Resource layer** — Fire-sector resources (your own and
  peers').
- **Weather layer** — fire-weather signals (wind, fuel
  moisture, fire danger ratings).
- **Infrastructure layer** — water sources, helibases, fire
  trails, structures.

The workspace is read for situational awareness and write
for capture / acknowledgement. State-changing actions
beyond capture are reserved for the sector's command chain.

## 4. Capturing signals well

Your captures feed the agents' models and the operating
picture. Be specific:
- *"Fire-front 50 m west of Khancoban pumper, 2 m/s
  east-south-east, 30 m flame length, head fire on the
  Khancoban-Cabramurra road"* not *"big fire near
  Khancoban"*.
- Stamp time and location (defaults to *now* and GPS).
- Photograph if safe.
- Tag your confidence in the observation.

## 5. Status updates

Update your status when it changes materially:
- Location change (your crew is moving to a new sector).
- Resource state change (water tank refilled / depleted;
  fuel low; equipment failure).
- Crew count change (additional resources joining; crew
  rotated out).
- Operational state change (responding / staging / engaged
  / recovering).

The audit trail records your status timeline; the operating
picture reflects your current state to upstream personas.

## 6. Receiving tasks

Tasks from your sector's Operations Officer arrive in your
inbox. For each task:
- Read.
- Acknowledge or query.
- Execute.
- Update status as you progress.
- Mark complete when done.

For tasks you cannot execute (resource gap, conflict with
existing assignment), query promptly via the workspace's
*Query* affordance; don't sit on it.

## 7. Evacuation directives

When Field Coordinator declares an evacuation envelope that
includes your forward position or affects your operating
area, the directive arrives in your workspace banner.
Acknowledge promptly. The Hub records your acknowledgement
as part of the evacuation's audit trail.

## 8. Working offline

The offline model is documented in
[`personas/field-coordinator`](/help/personas/field-coordinator).
Your front-line role uses the same model:
- Service worker registered.
- Critical actions queue locally.
- Queue replays on reconnection.
- Tile cache for your reachable radius.

## 9. Common pitfalls

### 9.1 Slow status updates
Stale status updates erode the operating picture. Update
when state changes materially, not just at shift transitions.

### 9.2 Vague signal captures
*"Big fire"* doesn't help the agents. Be specific.

### 9.3 Acknowledging directives without verification
A directive that includes a forward position you can't
verify is occupied requires direct observation before
acknowledging.

### 9.4 Off-sector queries via Hub
For cross-sector queries (e.g., requesting medical support),
go through the cross-sector chat or via your Operations
Officer; don't try to action from front-line.
