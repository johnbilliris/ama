# Observer / Auditor — getting started

The **Observer / Auditor** is the AMA Hub's read-only, full-
visibility persona. Observer mode is used by post-incident
review teams, AAO scrutiny, executive oversight, content
authors verifying doctrinal change end-to-end, and standing
governance committees. The Observer's audit weight is high
because the findings they record become the formal scrutiny
record for the tenant.

This article gets a brand-new Observer productive in fifteen
minutes; for the deep dive on the mode, read
[`observer-mode-guide`](/help/articles/observer-mode-guide).

## 1. Your envelope at a glance

You can:
- **Read** every operating workspace.
- **Read** all confidence bands including red.
- **Read** the audit timeline in full, including refusal rows
  and DLQ entries.
- **Read** every artefact's pre-state and post-state snapshots.
- **Read** snapshot history and configuration changes.
- **Record** findings against any audit row in your scrutiny
  scope.
- **Export** evidence packs for your scrutiny scope.
- **Hand off** scrutiny tickets to peer Observers across
  shifts.

You cannot:
- Take any state-changing action — no approvals, publications,
  resource moves, threshold tuning, claim changes, calibration
  refits.
- Author or edit help articles.
- Issue chat prompts that would invoke the gateway's deny path
  (Observer's gateway profile is the strictest available).
- Read content outside your scrutiny scope without expanding
  the scope (which prompts a confirmation dialog and audit-
  logs the expansion).

## 2. First fifteen minutes on shift

1. **Sign in** with your `observer-auditor` claim. Land at the
   audit timeline.
2. **Verify the *Observer* watermark** is present on every
   page. If not, raise an incident — constitutional gate
   breach.
3. **Open the active scrutiny ticket** if one exists; if not,
   create one for your shift's purpose at
   `/governance/scrutiny/new`.
4. **Set the scrutiny scope** — persona / jurisdiction / time
   window appropriate to your purpose.
5. **Open the Governance Dashboard** (`g g`). Note the
   constitutional gates' state at scrutiny start.
6. **Verbal handover** with outgoing Observer if the scrutiny
   is multi-shift.

## 3. The scrutiny workflow

See [`observer-mode-guide`](/help/articles/observer-mode-guide)
for the full walkthrough. The Observer's typical sequence:

1. Define scope (persona / jurisdiction / time window).
2. Walk the audit timeline forward, reading each row in
   detail.
3. For each row, consult:
   - The structured payload.
   - The pre-state and post-state snapshots.
   - The correlation graph (the DAG of related rows).
   - The doctrinal snapshot in force at action time.
4. Verify the hash chain (each row's chain dot should be
   green; red is a constitutional incident).
5. Record findings as you go (`n` shortcut on a focused row).
6. At scrutiny close, generate an evidence pack from the
   ticket.

## 4. Findings are first-class

Findings have kinds:
- **Observation** — a noted fact, no judgement.
- **Concern** — a noted fact that the Observer believes
  warrants attention.
- **Recommendation** — a proposed change to doctrine, content,
  or process.
- **Compliance gap** — a noted breach of a doctrinal or
  regulatory requirement.
- **Commendation** — a noted positive (use these; they
  balance the scrutiny record).

Rationale is **not** PII-redacted in findings; it goes into the
evidence pack verbatim. Write knowing it will be read by
external reviewers.

## 5. Handing off scrutiny

Long-running scrutinies span shifts. The hand-off workflow:
- Choose the receiving Observer from a dropdown of currently
  signed-in `observer-auditor` personas.
- Add a hand-off note summarising findings to date, open
  lines of inquiry, suggested next steps.
- The receiving Observer's banner shows the hand-off; on
  acceptance the ticket is theirs and your view becomes
  read-only on the ticket.
- Hand-off is reversible by mutual *Return ticket*.

## 6. Working with the Help Assistant in Observer mode

The Observer's chat assistant is grounded against the help
corpus *and* the audit-row content within your scrutiny
scope. You can ask, e.g., *"Why was warning W-2026-0193
revised after publication?"*, and the assistant cites the
revision audit row inline.

The assistant's grounding is bounded by your scope; widening
the scope expands grounding (with the audit-logged
confirmation).

Observer prompts are recorded with scrutiny ticket metadata so
external reviewers can later see what lines of inquiry were
pursued.

## 7. Cross-tenant scrutiny

The Observer/Auditor envelope is by default tenant-scoped.
Cross-tenant scrutiny (e.g., a NEMA-led review of a multi-
state event) is governed by a NEMA-level ACL and is granted
on a per-incident basis; in-app there is no path to expand
beyond your tenant.

## 8. Common pitfalls

### 8.1 Scrutiny without scope
Walking the timeline without a defined scope produces
diffuse findings that don't map to any review's purpose.
Always set scope first.

### 8.2 Findings without rationale
A finding with no rationale is just a flag; the rationale is
the value. Spend the time.

### 8.3 Skipping commendations
Scrutiny records that contain only concerns and gaps produce
defensive postures in the audited team. Find the things
that went well; record them.

### 8.4 Scope expansion without intent
Expanding scope prompts a confirmation; expanding casually
to "look around" inflates the audit record. Expand
deliberately.

### 8.5 Treating Observer as spectator
Observer is the scrutiny instrument. Read deliberately;
record purposefully; produce evidence packs that downstream
reviewers can use.
