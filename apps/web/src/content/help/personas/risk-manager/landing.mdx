# Risk Manager — getting started

The **Risk Manager (RM)** is the AMA Hub's confidence-band
steward and the only operating persona who can see red-band
signals. The RM tunes the agent's calibration, promotes /
demotes signals across bands, runs the threshold tuning
workflow, and acts as the Hub's connection between empirical
hit-rate reality and the operating personas' decision-rights
envelopes.

This article gets a brand-new RM productive in fifteen minutes
and documents the envelope, the calibration workflow, the
promotion / demotion doctrine, and the most common pitfalls.

## 1. Your envelope at a glance

You can:
- **See** red-band signals (suppressed for every operating
  persona other than you, Observer/Auditor, and Executive
  Leader).
- **Promote** red signals to amber after manual review.
- **Demote** green/amber signals when contradicting evidence
  emerges.
- **Tune** confidence-band thresholds within the doctrinal
  floors and ceilings.
- **Force calibration refit** from the prior 90 days of
  ground-truth labels.
- **Read** all bands of all artefacts.
- **Co-sign** amber actions when the operating chain runs out
  of authorised co-signers (rare delegation pattern).

You cannot:
- Approve public warnings.
- Issue tasks (Operations Officer's role).
- Allocate resources (Logistics Officer's role).
- Take state-changing operational actions outside the
  band-management workflow.

## 2. First fifteen minutes on shift

1. **Sign in**; you land at the Risk workspace.
2. **Open the calibration panel** on the Governance
   Dashboard. Read the reliability plot. Note the fit
   timestamp.
3. **Open the Risk-assessment agent workspace**. Read the
   composite scores; note any > 15.
4. **Read pending promotion / demotion requests** from
   operating personas (the workspace surfaces these in a
   side rail).
5. **Read the threshold-tuning history**. Note any recent
   changes and their rationale.
6. **Verbal handover** with outgoing RM. Confirm any pending
   promotions in flight or steward acknowledgements awaited.

## 3. The calibration workflow

See [`playbooks/agent-risk-assessment`](/help/articles/playbooks/agent-risk-assessment)
for the playbook. The RM's calibration responsibilities:

- **Daily glance** at the reliability plot. Drift from the
  diagonal is the diagnostic.
- **Refit** when the plot has visibly drifted; refit pulls
  the prior 90 days of ground-truth labels and re-fits the
  isotonic regression.
- **Tune thresholds** when refit doesn't restore alignment,
  or when the operational tempo demands different
  cost-balance points. Tuning is bounded
  (0.50–0.60 amber/red; 0.80–0.90 green/amber) and requires
  constitutional steward acknowledgement.

## 4. Promotion and demotion

See [`amber-confidence-badge`](/help/articles/amber-confidence-badge)
for the band doctrine and
[`playbooks/agent-risk-assessment`](/help/articles/playbooks/agent-risk-assessment)
for the playbook. The RM's process:

- **Promote** a red signal to amber when manual review and at
  least one independent source corroborate operational
  significance. Rationale required; reversible within 30 min.
- **Demote** when contradicting evidence emerges.
- Be slow to promote; each promotion erodes the agent's
  red-band suppression model.

## 5. Threshold tuning

The threshold tuning panel is the RM's most consequential
tool. The workflow:
1. Drag the proposed thresholds.
2. *Preview impact*; the Hub replays 30 days under the
   proposed thresholds.
3. *Apply*; constitutional steward acknowledgement is
   required.
4. The new thresholds take effect immediately; audit-logged
   with both the RM and steward identities.

Don't tune speculatively; tune in response to a specific
calibration-or-cost-balance signal.

## 6. The constitutional steward relationship

The constitutional steward is the doctrinal authority on
band doctrine. The RM and steward have a working
relationship:
- Steward acknowledges threshold tuning.
- RM consults steward on novel promotion patterns.
- Steward records exception ledger entries when RM-steward
  consultation produces a doctrinal-precedent decision.

Cultivate this relationship; the doctrinal soundness of
your tenant's band doctrine depends on it.

## 7. Working with operating personas

Operating personas can't see red signals; they can request
promotion. Promotion requests appear in your workspace
side-rail. Process:
- Read the requesting persona's rationale.
- Cross-check the requested signal's source and
  corroboration.
- Either *Promote* (with your own rationale) or *Decline*
  (with rationale).

A pattern of repeated promotion requests for similar signals
indicates a possible threshold mis-calibration; consider
tuning.

## 8. Common pitfalls

### 8.1 Promotion inflation
Promoting freely turns the band model into a theatre of
amber. Be slow; demand corroboration; document rationale.

### 8.2 Stale calibration
Day-by-day, calibration ages. Don't let it cross 60 days
without a refit; the staleness penalty cascades through every
operating persona's actions.

### 8.3 Tuning as workflow workaround
*"Operations is asking for too much amber co-sign; let me
tune the threshold up"* is a doctrinal abuse. Tune for
empirical reasons, not workflow comfort.

### 8.4 Skipping the steward acknowledgement
The acknowledgement step is constitutional. Don't try to
work around it; the audit trail and the gate enforcement
will catch you.

### 8.5 Detachment from operations
The RM is sometimes seen as off-floor. Stay engaged via the
cross-sector chat and via periodic sit-ins on the Operations
workspace; calibration should respond to operational
reality.
