# Operations Officer — getting started

The **Operations Officer (OO)** is the AIIMS Operations function
in the AMA Hub. Where the Incident Commander makes the
authority calls, the OO turns those calls into co-ordinated
action: tasks issued, resources deployed, sectors briefed,
decisions logged, escalations forwarded. The OO is the
**busiest persona during active operations** — most amber
co-signs, most cross-sector chats, most workspace traffic.

This article gets a brand-new OO productive in fifteen minutes
and documents the envelope, the typical-shift arc, the
co-sign chain points, and the most common pitfalls.

## 1. Your envelope at a glance

You can:
- **Issue tasks** to sectors and front-line personas within
  your jurisdictions.
- **Brief sectors** via the cross-sector workspace.
- **Forward agent recommendations** to Logistics Officer (for
  resource allocation) or Field Coordinator (for evacuation
  preparation).
- **Co-sign** Logistics Officer transfer co-sign chains; PIO
  alerts in many cases (with IC as final co-signer for
  emergency severity).
- **Log decisions** to the Decision Log (workspace tile).
- **Pause** agents (Monitoring, Risk-assessment, Evacuation,
  Resource-allocation, Communication) within your tenant.
- **Initiate** NEMA acknowledgement for cross-jurisdictional
  effects you cause.

You cannot:
- Approve public warnings as final co-signer (IC's role).
- Tune confidence-band thresholds (Risk Manager's role).
- Take state-changing actions outside your jurisdictions
  without NEMA acknowledgement.

## 2. First fifteen minutes on shift

1. **Sign in**; you land at the Operations workspace
   (`/workspaces/operations`).
2. **Glance the AU-region banner**.
3. **Scan your inbox** — pending co-sign requests, pending
   task acknowledgements from sectors.
4. **Open the Monitoring agent workspace** (`g w`, `m`). Read
   the top recommendations.
5. **Open the Disaster Map**. Take in the operating picture.
6. **Open the Decision Log**. Read the last shift's
   decisions and rationale.
7. **Verbal handover** with outgoing OO. Confirm in-flight
   workflows.
8. **Open the Resource board** (`/workspaces/logistics` —
   read-only for OO). Get a sense of resource posture.

## 3. The OO's typical shift arc

- **Quiet** — task tidying, decision-log review, exercise
  participation if scheduled, cross-sector chat catch-up.
- **Watch and act** — increased agent recommendation forwarding,
  pre-positioning resource recommendations, sector briefings.
- **Emergency warning** — co-sign chain dominates. Expect
  interruptions every minute or two; cross-sector chat is
  busy; the Decision Log fills rapidly.

## 4. Issuing tasks

Tasks go through the Operations workspace's task affordance:
- Compose the task (sector, action, deadline, any constraints).
- Assign to a front-line persona or to a sector inbox.
- Send. The recipient acknowledges (or queries; or declines
  with rationale).
- Audit-logged on every state change.

For multi-step or multi-sector tasks, use the **task
template** library — pre-built sequences for common operational
patterns (initial assessment, evacuation envelope reconnaissance,
warning verification, resource pre-position).

## 5. Co-signing well

The OO co-signs at three points:
- **Logistics Officer transfers** — verify the originating
  Logistics Officer's draft; check the mutual-aid agreement
  is active; check the receiving sector's notional capacity.
- **PIO alerts** (when severity is amber-co-sign-required and
  IC is unavailable; rare path, only when explicitly
  delegated). Verify the negative-evidence acknowledgements.
- **Field Coordinator declarations** (informational; you
  signal acceptance of the envelope into the operating
  posture).

Doctrine for co-signing well is the same as for IC: read the
evidence, verify the snapshot, prefer hand-off to delay.

## 6. Cross-sector coordination

The OO is the cross-sector hinge. The cross-sector workspace
(`/workspaces/cross-sector`) shows each sector's status; the
cross-sector chat is the OO's primary out-of-Hub-equivalent
coordination channel.

Patterns:
- **Joint task** — issue a single task with co-ordinated
  sector-level sub-tasks; the workspace surfaces the
  composition.
- **Sector briefing** — a structured narrative posted to the
  cross-sector chat with relevant evidence cards attached;
  recipient sectors acknowledge.
- **Conflict resolution** — when sectors propose conflicting
  actions, the OO chairs the resolution; the agreed action is
  audit-logged with all sector heads.

## 7. The Decision Log

Every significant operational decision should be logged. The
Decision Log captures:
- The decision (e.g., *"Pre-position two heavy tankers at
  Tumbarumba before the wind shift"*).
- The rationale.
- The alternatives considered.
- The persona(s) involved.
- The expected effect and the success criteria.
- A *review-by* time at which the decision should be
  re-examined.

Decisions are audit-logged. They are the post-event review's
primary narrative anchor.

## 8. Cross-jurisdictional effects

Same workflow as IC: the NEMA acknowledgement dialog
appears for actions whose effect crosses subdivisions. As OO
you typically choose *Notify and proceed* (your authority is
intra-state) and let IC consider *Hand off* if the
character is federal.

## 9. Common pitfalls

### 9.1 Skipping the Decision Log
A shift with sparse Decision Log entries is harder to debrief.
Reviewers can read the audit trail but the *why* is in the
Decision Log.

### 9.2 Over-issuing tasks
A backlog of unacknowledged tasks creates noise. Issue
deliberately; verify acknowledgement; close completed tasks.

### 9.3 Co-signing as rubber-stamp
The audit captures you as a peer. Read the originating
evidence; don't co-sign on velocity alone.

### 9.4 Cross-sector chat as primary
The cross-sector chat is for coordination, not for primary
task issuance. Tasks go through the task affordance so they
have proper structure and tracking.

### 9.5 Persona mixing mid-shift
Don't switch into Logistics Officer mid-shift just to "see
their view"; use the Resource board's read-only view from
the OO workspace instead.
