# Commonwealth — getting started

The **Commonwealth** persona is the federal-level oversight
and coordination role in the AMA Hub: NEMA controllers, Bureau
of Meteorology liaison officers at the Commonwealth level,
Geoscience Australia liaison, and the Department of Foreign
Affairs and Trade for international assistance. The
Commonwealth persona provides cross-jurisdictional
acknowledgement authority, federal-character action handling,
and the diplomatic channels for international resource
movement.

This article gets a brand-new Commonwealth controller
productive in fifteen minutes.

## 1. Your envelope at a glance

You can:
- **Read** every federated tenant's operating picture
  (subject to the standing federation agreements).
- **Acknowledge** cross-jurisdictional actions notified by
  state-level personas.
- **Receive** handed-off federal-character actions and act on
  them as if you held the originating state-level envelope.
- **Coordinate** international assistance via the OS
  jurisdiction code.
- **Issue** Commonwealth-level postures and bulletins.
- **Co-sign** for actions the standing agreements require
  Commonwealth involvement on (e.g., MFA-touching
  international resource transfers).

You cannot:
- Take state-level operational actions on a state's behalf
  unless explicitly handed off.
- Tune state-level confidence-band thresholds.
- Override state-level constitutional gate states (each
  tenant's gates are tenant-scoped).

## 2. First fifteen minutes on shift

1. **Sign in**; you land at the Governance Dashboard
   (Commonwealth tenant).
2. **Read the federation posture panel**. Note any tenants
   with stale handshakes.
3. **Read pending NEMA acknowledgement requests** in your
   inbox.
4. **Read pending federal-character handoffs** awaiting your
   action.
5. **Read the Commonwealth-tenant audit timeline** for the
   last hour.
6. **Verbal handover** with outgoing Commonwealth controller.

## 3. NEMA acknowledgement

State-level operators initiating cross-jurisdictional actions
present the *NEMA acknowledgement* dialog. Their two choices:
- *Notify NEMA and proceed* — the action commits with you
  notified asynchronously. Your inbox shows the notification;
  you don't co-sign, but you do see it.
- *Hand off to NEMA* — the action queues for your action.
  You see it as an actionable item.

For *Notify and proceed*: read; verify; if the action is
sound, no further action required from you. If the action
warrants Commonwealth attention, engage the originating IC
verbally.

For *Hand off*: act. Your action commits as if you held the
originating state's envelope; the audit row records you as
actor and the originator as the requester.

## 4. Federal-character actions

Federal-character actions are those whose doctrinal authority
clearly rests at the Commonwealth level:
- **Multi-state resource coordination** (e.g., DEFGLOG
  activations).
- **International assistance** (sending Australian resources
  to Pacific neighbours; receiving international
  assistance).
- **Federal-asset deployment** (ADF in support of civilian
  authorities; AMSA marine rescue at federal direction).
- **Cross-jurisdictional public communication** at the
  federal level (e.g., national-scale advisories).

These are typically *Hand off to NEMA* actions, but state-
level controllers occasionally take them via *Notify and
proceed* when speed matters and the NEMA on-call is
engaged elsewhere.

## 5. International assistance

For OS jurisdiction (Pacific assistance):
- The diplomatic note from MFA (DFAT) is the prerequisite.
- The Hub's recommendations cite the diplomatic note number.
- Customs and biosecurity are co-ordinated with border
  agencies; the Hub doesn't manage these but you are the
  liaison.
- Expect 24-hour-plus turnaround.

The OS jurisdiction code in the multi-jurisdiction filter
includes Pacific neighbours. State-level operators who do
not hold the OS claim cannot operate in OS; you are the
typical actor for OS-scoped artefacts.

## 6. Commonwealth bulletins

The Commonwealth tenant publishes bulletins (national-scale
posture, emergency declaration notices, ministerial
statements). The bulletin composer is at
`/commonwealth/bulletins`; co-sign chain is Commonwealth
controller + Commonwealth executive (typically the on-call
emergency management deputy secretary).

Bulletins reach federated state tenants as advisory rows in
their workspace banners.

## 7. Working with state-level personas

The Commonwealth persona's relationship with state-level
operating personas is collaborative, not directive. Engage:
- Verbally / via phone for time-sensitive coordination.
- Via the cross-tenant chat (federation panel).
- Via the NEMA acknowledgement workflow for formal records.

Don't try to direct state-level operations from the
Commonwealth tenant; the doctrinal authority is the state's.

## 8. Common pitfalls

### 8.1 Acknowledgement queue inflation
*Notify and proceed* notifications accumulate in your
inbox. Triage them — read, judge, acknowledge as seen,
escalate the few that warrant attention.

### 8.2 Federal-character drift
Some hand-off requests are state-character actions that the
state controller wants to share blame for. Recognise the
pattern; return to the state controller for state-level
action when appropriate.

### 8.3 International assistance without diplomatic note
The diplomatic note is the prerequisite, not a formality.
Don't initiate OS-jurisdiction action without confirming
the note number.

### 8.4 Federation handshake neglect
A federation handshake going stale silently degrades cross-
tenant coordination. Watch the federation panel; engage the
affected tenant's executive.
