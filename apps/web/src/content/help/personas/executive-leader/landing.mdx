# Executive Leader — getting started

The **Executive Leader (EL)** is the AMA Hub's executive
oversight persona: agency commissioners, jurisdiction-level
emergency controllers, NEMA executive, government department
leads. The EL's envelope is **read-only with publication
authority**: they do not take operational actions, but they
sign off on snapshot publications, posture exports, and the
formal posture briefings that go to government.

This article gets a brand-new EL productive in fifteen
minutes.

## 1. Your envelope at a glance

You can:
- **Read** the Governance Dashboard end-to-end.
- **Read** the audit timeline (your tenant scope).
- **Read** all confidence bands including red.
- **Co-sign** help-snapshot publications (with Risk Manager).
- **Export** posture snapshots for briefings.
- **Export** evidence packs for executive review.
- **Acknowledge** constitutional gates after steward
  remediation (in some tenants this authority is
  shared with the steward).

You cannot:
- Take any operational state-changing action.
- Tune confidence-band thresholds.
- Author or edit help articles.
- Approve public warnings.

## 2. First fifteen minutes on shift

1. **Sign in**; you land at the Governance Dashboard.
2. **Read the constitutional gates panel**. Note any amber
   or red gates.
3. **Read the calibration panel**. Note the fit timestamp.
4. **Read the audit pipeline panel**. Note throughput,
   latency, DLQ depth.
5. **Read the localisation panel**. Note any languages with
   coverage gaps.
6. **Read the snapshot freshness panel**. Note when the next
   publication is scheduled.
7. **Read the federation posture panel** for cross-tenant
   reachability.
8. **Read the audit timeline** for the last hour to get a
   sense of operational tempo.
9. **Verbal handover** with outgoing EL if applicable.

## 3. The dashboard as briefing source

The Governance Dashboard is your primary briefing source.
The *Export posture* button emits a signed posture snapshot
suitable for ministerial briefings, AAO submissions, or
inter-agency reports. The export contains:
- Constitutional gates state and recent state changes.
- Calibration health summary.
- Audit pipeline health summary.
- Localisation coverage summary.
- Snapshot freshness.
- Jurisdictional KPIs.
- Federation posture.

The export is signed; reviewers can verify against the Hub's
published signing key.

## 4. Snapshot publication co-sign

Help-snapshot publications require Executive Leader + Risk
Manager co-sign. The workflow:
- Risk Manager initiates *Publish now* from the Governance
  Dashboard's snapshot panel.
- The Hub presents the snapshot's manifest (article diffs,
  doctrine changes, calibration impact projection).
- EL reviews and co-signs.
- Snapshot publishes; audit-logged with both identities.

The co-sign is doctrinally significant — the snapshot is the
in-force doctrine for every action audit-logged thereafter.
Don't co-sign without reading the manifest.

## 5. Constitutional gate acknowledgement

When a constitutional gate goes red, the steward remediates;
some tenants require EL acknowledgement to clear the gate.
The acknowledgement records the EL's name, the gate, the
remediation rationale, and the post-acknowledgement state.

Don't acknowledge without satisfying yourself that the
remediation is sound. The audit trail captures EL
acknowledgement as a doctrinal-precedent decision.

## 6. Working with operations

The EL is not on the operations floor. Engage via:
- The cross-sector chat for situational briefings (read-only).
- Periodic visits to active operations workspaces.
- Post-event evidence-pack reviews with the IC and Operations
  Officer.

Don't try to direct operations from the executive layer; the
operating personas hold the doctrine.

## 7. Inter-tenant relationships

The federation panel shows cross-tenant reachability. EL
relationships across tenants matter:
- Coordinate with peer Executive Leaders before joint
  posture exports.
- Engage the on-call NEMA controller for federation
  reachability concerns.
- Pre-stage cross-border briefings through the federation
  channels.

## 8. Common pitfalls

### 8.1 Co-signing snapshot without reading
The manifest is dense; reading takes time; the doctrinal
weight is high. Make the time.

### 8.2 Acknowledging gate without remediation review
EL acknowledgement is the constitutional close-out; if the
remediation isn't sound, the close-out is hollow. Insist on
adequate evidence.

### 8.3 Posture export without context
A posture snapshot exported without an accompanying
narrative is hard to read. Couple with a written brief
authored by you.

### 8.4 Operational interference
The EL is not the operations director. Stay in the
oversight envelope; trust IC and Operations.
