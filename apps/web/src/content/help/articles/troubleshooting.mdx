# Troubleshooting & FAQ

This is the consolidated incident catalogue for operators. Each section
lists the symptom you will actually see in the UI, the likely cause, the
self-service remediation, and — if the issue is not self-recoverable —
the escalation path. Every remediation that touches a state-changing
operation is audit-logged; remediations are not a back-door.

If you are reading this *during* an incident and you cannot proceed,
skip directly to **§12 SupportHandoff** at the bottom and start the
escalation while you read.

## 1. Sign-in and identity

### 1.1 "Sign-in refused: tenant not permitted"
Your IdP tenant is not federated with the Hub. The Hub permits exactly
two classes of tenant: the AGW (Australian Government) tenant and any
state/territory tenant that has signed a Tenant Federation Agreement
with NEMA. Self-service is impossible.

**Remediation:** forward the on-page correlation ID to your agency
administrator. Do not screenshot the URL — the correlation ID alone is
sufficient and avoids exposing query-string PII.

### 1.2 "403 — your account has no Hub role"
Your identity is recognised but no Hub persona claim is present.

**Remediation:** your administrator must add you to the appropriate
persona security group in your tenant. The Hub re-reads claims on every
sign-in; once added, sign out fully (`/auth/signout`) and back in. Group
propagation can take up to fifteen minutes.

### 1.3 "Session expired" loop
You are stuck in a redirect loop between `/auth/callback` and `/`.

**Cause:** clock skew between your device and the IdP. The JWT `nbf`
claim is being rejected as in-the-future.

**Remediation:** sync your device clock to NTP. On Windows: open
Settings → Time & Language → Date & Time → Sync now. On macOS the system
clock is automatic but check that you are on AEST/AEDT, not the Pacific
default. Repeat sign-in.

### 1.4 "Persona claim ambiguous"
You hold more than one persona but your IdP did not nominate a default.

**Remediation:** the persona switcher will appear with no selection.
Choose the persona appropriate to your current shift; the choice is
audit-logged. Ask your administrator to set the `default_persona` claim
attribute so the choice is stable across sessions.

## 2. Persona switching

### 2.1 Switcher is read-only
You hold a single persona claim. The switcher is intentionally disabled —
session-scoped persona elevation would defeat the constitutional
decision-rights envelope (FR-016, spec-035).

**Remediation:** request a second persona via your administrator. There
is no in-app workaround.

### 2.2 Switching persona does not change the page chrome
You changed persona on a screen that is *not* persona-scoped (e.g.,
`/help`, `/governance/audit`). The chrome will refresh on the next
persona-scoped navigation.

**Remediation:** navigate to a workspace (e.g., `/workspaces`) to see the
new persona's surfaces.

### 2.3 "Persona switch refused: shadow exercise active"
You are inside a Simulation tenant where switching persona would change
your in-exercise role mid-flight. This is intentional — exercise replay
must reproduce the same actor envelope at every step.

**Remediation:** pause the exercise (Simulation Controller → Pause), or
hand the controller role to another participant before switching.

## 3. Jurisdiction filters

### 3.1 "No data for this filter"
Your filter narrowed the scope to zero permitted jurisdictions. Common
when an operator first sees the Hub on a fresh device — the local default
is often more restrictive than the IdP claim.

**Remediation:** click **Reset to claim union** in the filter pop-over.

### 3.2 Filter resets when I switch persona
Some personas have a *constrained* default filter (e.g., a Sector
front-line persona is locked to that sector's home jurisdiction). The
constraint is part of the persona's decision-rights envelope.

**Remediation:** none — this is by design. Use the parent jurisdiction
persona (e.g., Operations Officer) for cross-sector views.

### 3.3 "Cross-border action requires NEMA acknowledgement"
You attempted a state-changing action whose effect crosses jurisdiction
boundaries. The Hub requires Commonwealth (`CW`) acknowledgement before
proceeding.

**Remediation:** the dialog offers two options: *Notify NEMA and proceed*
(records a notification and fires the action) or *Hand off to NEMA*
(transfers the decision to a Commonwealth controller). Both are
audit-logged.

## 4. Confidence bands and red-band suppression

### 4.1 No red-band signals visible
Red-band signals (`< 0.55` model confidence) are hard-suppressed in every
operating workspace except **Risk Manager**.

**Remediation:** if you are a Risk Manager, toggle *Show red-band* in the
layer switcher. If you are any other persona, the suppression is
intentional — request a Risk Manager review.

### 4.2 Amber band feels too aggressive
Your tenant's amber threshold is configured by the Risk Manager. Defaults
are 0.55 (lower) and 0.85 (upper). See the section on threshold tuning
in [`screen-by-screen/governance-dashboard`](/help/articles/screen-by-screen/governance-dashboard).

## 5. Approvals and warnings

### 5.1 "Approval refused: outside decision-rights envelope"
Your persona is not authorised for the proposed action. The dialog will
name the persona that *is* authorised.

**Remediation:** hand off via *Send to <persona>*. The handoff is
audit-logged and the receiver is notified in their workspace banner.

### 5.2 Warning sits in "Pending publication"
A warning has been approved but the public-channel adapter is offline or
the Bureau has rate-limited the channel. The Hub queues warnings safely;
they will publish when the channel recovers.

**Remediation:** check `/governance/dashboard` → *Outbound channels*. If
red, raise an incident with your channel administrator.

### 5.3 "Stale evidence: signal age > 60 minutes"
The evidence supporting an approval has aged out of the freshness band.

**Remediation:** refresh the evidence panel (`F5` on the evidence card)
to re-pull the signal. If the signal source is itself stale, the
underlying issue is upstream and the Hub will not re-stamp.

## 6. Audit trail

### 6.1 "Audit row not yet visible"
The audit pipeline is asynchronous; expect up to thirty seconds latency
between an action and its appearance on `/governance/audit`. If a row
does not appear within five minutes, raise an incident — that is a
constitutional gate breach.

### 6.2 "Audit export refused: tenant scope mismatch"
You attempted to export an evidence pack for a tenant you do not have
Observer/Auditor or Executive Leader rights on.

**Remediation:** request the appropriate role. Cross-tenant audit access
is governed by NEMA-level ACL and is never granted in-app.

### 6.3 Audit DLQ shows non-zero
The audit dead-letter queue surfaces rows that failed to chain
cryptographically. This is a constitutional alarm.

**Remediation:** stop state-changing work for your tenant immediately.
Raise an incident referencing the DLQ entry IDs. The constitutional
steward will determine whether to invoke recovery.

## 7. Chat refusals

The Help Assistant refuses for a closed set of reasons. Each refusal
explains itself; this section covers the categories.

### 7.1 "Out of scope"
You asked about something not in the help corpus — typically operational
data, real-time intelligence, or off-platform topics.

**Remediation:** consult the underlying workspace (e.g., for live signals,
go to the Disaster Map). The assistant will never speculate.

### 7.2 "Sensitive operational"
Your prompt referenced PSPF-marked or sensitive-operational content. The
gateway is enforcing FR-RAG-NS rules.

**Remediation:** rephrase to avoid sensitive specifics, or escalate to
the cleared-channel briefing tool.

### 7.3 "PII detected"
Your prompt or the candidate response contains personally identifiable
information. The gateway redacts before grounding.

**Remediation:** the redacted answer is still delivered; a notice
explains *what* was redacted. If the redaction is over-eager, raise a
content review via the article's feedback bar.

### 7.4 "Region not permitted"
The deployment region for the chat agent is not in the AU allow-list.
This should never happen in production.

**Remediation:** stop using the assistant; raise an incident immediately.

## 8. Offline / degraded mode

### 8.1 "Offline — N actions queued"
Connectivity is lost. The Hub continues to accept signal-capture, status
updates, and evacuation acknowledgements; everything else is read-only.

**Remediation:** none required. The queue replays automatically when
connectivity returns. If you absolutely must drop a queued action,
expand the banner, click the row, and confirm — the drop is audit-logged.

### 8.2 Map tiles look low-resolution
You are viewing a region whose tiles are not in the offline cache. The
Hub falls back to a low-fidelity raster.

**Remediation:** when connectivity returns, scroll the affected region;
the cache refreshes opportunistically.

### 8.3 "Stale tile · last refresh DD/MM"
The tile cache has not been refreshed within the freshness band.

**Remediation:** allow the page to sit in the foreground for ~30 seconds
to give the cache refresh time to complete. If the stamp does not update,
your network egress is being filtered; check with your IT team.

## 9. Performance

### 9.1 Map pan/zoom is sluggish
Likely cause: too many simultaneous signal layers enabled. Each layer
adds a Cosmos point-query subscription.

**Remediation:** reduce layers in the layer switcher. Persistent
sluggishness with the default layer set indicates a backend issue —
raise an incident.

### 9.2 Chat responses take > 30 seconds
The chat agent is rate-limited or the model deployment is degraded. The
SSE stream will eventually complete; do not refresh.

**Remediation:** if you absolutely cannot wait, click *Cancel*; the
turn is audit-logged as cancelled. Re-prompt with the same question; the
assistant treats re-prompts deterministically.

### 9.3 Workspace tiles show stale numbers
The workspace polls Cosmos every eight seconds. If a tile is more than
sixty seconds stale, it overlays a *"stale"* badge.

**Remediation:** click the badge to force-refresh. Persistent staleness
across all tiles indicates a backend issue.

## 10. Browser and device

### 10.1 "Service worker registration failed"
Offline mode requires a service worker. Some browser privacy settings
(strict tracking prevention) block registration.

**Remediation:** allow service workers for the Hub origin. The Hub does
not use service workers for tracking — only for the offline queue and
tile cache.

### 10.2 "WebGL not available"
The map renderer requires WebGL 2.0. Headless browsers and remote-
desktop sessions over compressed WAN often disable WebGL.

**Remediation:** enable hardware acceleration in your browser. If you
are on Citrix or AVD, request a GPU-enabled host pool from your IT team.

### 10.3 Pop-up blocker eats the SupportHandoff window
The handoff opens in a new tab to keep the audit context intact.

**Remediation:** allow pop-ups for the Hub origin.

## 11. Constitutional gates

The ten constitutional gates are visible on `/governance/dashboard`. A red
gate suspends state-changing operations for your tenant.

| Gate | Failure symptom |
|------|-----------------|
| AU-region residency | "Action refused: residency gate red" |
| Append-only audit | "Action refused: audit gate red" |
| Persona decision-rights | "Action refused: persona gate red" |
| Jurisdiction scoping | "Action refused: jurisdiction gate red" |
| Confidence bands | "Action refused: confidence gate red" |
| Citation grounding | Help Assistant refuses with "grounding gate red" |
| Refusal hygiene | Chat panel disabled |
| Snapshot freshness | Article serves with "Snapshot stale" banner |
| Sensitive operational | Gateway escalates to deny |
| PII redaction | Gateway escalates to deny |

A red gate is **never** a workflow problem you can solve from the UI. It
indicates a constitutional breach and must be resolved by the
constitutional steward. Raise an incident immediately.

## 12. SupportHandoff (escalation)

When self-service is exhausted, click **Get help** at the bottom of any
help screen or chat panel. This composes an email to your tenant's
nominated support inbox containing:

- Your correlation ID (for log correlation).
- The redacted last-N turns of the chat (PII removed).
- The current persona, jurisdiction filter, and surface ID.
- The article slug you were reading, if any.

Your administrator configures the support inbox via
`PUT /api/help/support-handoff`. If the *Get help* button is missing or
red, the inbox is unconfigured — ask your administrator to set one.

## 13. Reporting a bug

Bugs are filed via the *Report a bug* link in the page footer. The form
attaches:

- Browser and OS strings.
- The last two console errors (sanitised).
- The last navigation event.
- Your correlation ID.

It does **not** attach screenshots automatically — if you want to
include one, attach it manually after sanitising it for PII.
