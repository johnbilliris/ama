# Getting started with the AMA Hub

The Autonomous Disaster Response Coordination Hub (the **Hub**) is a federated
operating picture for Australian disaster response. It brings together signals,
warnings, agent recommendations, jurisdictional data, governance posture, and
exercise replay into a single browser surface, while preserving AIDR doctrine,
AIIMS function discipline, and AU-only data residency at every layer.

This article gets a brand-new operator productive in roughly **fifteen minutes**.
Read end-to-end on first sign-in; thereafter use the right-rail Help Assistant
(`Ctrl+/` or `⌘+/`) for contextual answers grounded in this same corpus.

## 1. Before you sign in

You need three things from your home agency:

1. **An organisation identity** registered in the Australian Government's
   Microsoft Entra tenant, or in a tenant that has signed a federation
   agreement with NEMA. The Hub will not accept identities from non-AU tenants
   — this is enforced both at the Container Apps EasyAuth edge and again by
   the API's `EntraAuthMiddleware`. If you see *"Sign-in refused: tenant not
   permitted"*, your agency administrator needs to add the Hub's app
   registration to their tenant. There is no self-service path.
2. **At least one role assignment** mapped to the Hub's persona catalogue
   (Incident Commander, Operations Officer, Planning Officer, Field
   Coordinator, Public Information Officer, Logistics Officer, Resilience
   Planner, Risk Manager, Observer/Auditor, Executive Leader, or one of the
   front-line variants — Fire, Police, Medical, SES/Marine — plus the
   cross-sector and Commonwealth aggregations). Persona is computed from
   IdP claims (FR-027); no in-app override is possible without an audit
   record.
3. **A modern browser** — current Edge, Chrome, Firefox, or Safari (latest two
   minor versions). Internet Explorer and legacy Edge are not supported. The
   Hub is a single-page Next.js application; service-worker registration is
   required for offline mode.

If your jurisdiction operates in degraded connectivity (remote field
deployments, cyclone-affected regions), see the **Offline mode** section in
*Troubleshooting & FAQ* before your first deployment.

## 2. Signing in

1. Navigate to your agency's Hub URL (typically
   `https://hub.<your-agency>.gov.au`). All production deployments terminate
   on Azure Front Door in **Australia East**; you must not be tunnelling
   through a non-AU egress.
2. Choose **Sign in with organisation account**. The Hub redirects to your
   IdP; if you are already signed in to Microsoft 365, no password prompt
   appears.
3. On return, the Hub stamps a short-lived JWT, exchanges it for a tenant
   context, and redirects to the **Disaster Map** at `/`.

If sign-in completes but you land on a `403 Forbidden` page, your IdP
account is missing a role claim. Send the correlation ID shown on the page
to your agency administrator — it is a deterministic UUID derived from your
session and your tenant ID, and it is the fastest way for the support team
to reproduce the issue.

## 3. Your first sixty seconds on the Disaster Map

The **Disaster Map** (`/`) is the default landing surface for every persona
except Observer/Auditor (which lands on the Audit timeline).

- **Top-of-screen jurisdiction filter** — defaults to the union of every
  jurisdiction your IdP claim grants. Click to narrow. The filter is
  persisted to your `localStorage` per browser; it is *not* synced server-
  side, so a fresh device starts with the full claim union again.
- **Layer switcher** (right side) — toggle signals, warnings, agent
  recommendations, evacuation envelopes, and offline subsystem status.
  Each layer carries an AIDR / AIIMS provenance tag visible in the legend.
- **Confidence badges** on every signal pin: **green** ≥ 0.85, **amber**
  0.55–0.85, **red** < 0.55. Red is hard-suppressed unless the operator
  explicitly opts in via the **Risk Manager** workspace. See
  [`amber-confidence-badge`](/help/articles/amber-confidence-badge) for
  the full doctrine.
- **AU-region banner** in the bottom-left — always visible. If it ever
  reads anything other than *"Hosted in Australia · AU data residency"*,
  stop work and raise an incident.

## 4. Personas and workspaces

Personas in the Hub are not job titles — they are **decision-rights envelopes**
defined in the Constitution (`/help/articles/constitution-principle-v`).
Switching persona changes *what* you can see and approve, not just the
visual chrome.

| Persona                       | Default landing       | Decision rights                             |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Incident Commander            | Disaster Map          | Approve warnings, escalate cross-juris.     |
| Operations Officer            | Workspace · Operations | Issue tasks, brief sectors, log decisions. |
| Planning Officer              | Workspace · Planning   | Build IAPs, manage objectives.              |
| Public Information Officer    | Alert Composer        | Draft and submit public alerts.             |
| Logistics Officer             | Workspace · Logistics  | Resource allocation, reqs, approvals.      |
| Field Coordinator             | Workspace · Field      | Offline-first; signal capture.             |
| Risk Manager                  | Workspace · Risk       | Threshold tuning; red-band confidence.     |
| Resilience Planner            | Preparedness          | Pre-event plans, frameworks, gaps.          |
| Observer / Auditor            | Audit timeline        | Read-only; export evidence packs.           |
| Executive Leader              | Governance Dashboard  | Posture, KPIs, constitutional gates.        |
| Front-line (Fire/Police/Med/SES) | Sector workspace   | Sector-scoped operating views.              |

The **persona switcher** lives in the top-right of every page. Switching
records an audit row (`actor.persona_change`) so the audit trail captures
*who* held which envelope when each decision was made — this is FR-016 of
spec-035 and is a constitutional requirement.

If your IdP claim grants a single persona, the switcher is read-only. To
acquire a second persona you must have your agency administrator assign
the second role; the Hub will refuse session-scoped elevations.

## 5. Jurisdiction filters and AIIMS scoping

Jurisdiction filtering is **multiplicative** with persona scoping. An
Operations Officer with claim `["NSW", "VIC"]` who narrows the filter to
`NSW` will see only NSW-scoped signals *and* will only be able to issue
tasks to NSW resources. Re-broadening the filter does not retroactively
expand permissions on already-issued artefacts; the audit row records the
filter state at the moment of action.

Jurisdiction codes follow ISO-3166-2 AU subdivisions (`NSW`, `VIC`, `QLD`,
`WA`, `SA`, `TAS`, `NT`, `ACT`) plus three Hub-specific codes:
- `CW` — Commonwealth (NEMA, BoM, Geoscience Australia).
- `XB` — cross-border coordination cell.
- `OS` — overseas (Australian assistance to neighbours; Pacific lens only).

See [`screen-by-screen/multi-jurisdiction-filter`](/help/articles/screen-by-screen/multi-jurisdiction-filter)
for the full UI walkthrough.

## 6. The Help Assistant

A right-rail chat panel is always present on `/help` and on every screen
when you press `Ctrl+/`. The assistant is **grounded** — it cannot answer
from training data. Every response either cites an article from this
corpus or refuses with an explicit reason.

**What it will do**
- Answer "how do I…" questions about the Hub.
- Surface citations (article + anchor) you can click to open the source.
- Tell you which persona / jurisdiction / surface its answer assumed.
- Refuse politely if you ask about something outside the corpus.

**What it will not do**
- Summarise live operational data. The assistant does not see live signals;
  it sees only the published help corpus.
- Make decisions on your behalf. It will not approve warnings, dispatch
  resources, or change configuration. Those are reserved for the
  appropriate persona workspace.
- Speculate. If it has no grounding, it refuses.

Try these starter prompts (your prompts are audit-logged with persona +
jurisdiction context — phrase them professionally):

- "How do I escalate a NSW bushfire to NEMA when VIC is also affected?"
- "What does the amber confidence badge mean for an evacuation order?"
- "Where is the keyboard shortcut to open the audit timeline?"
- "Show me the playbook for an overnight communications outage."

If a refusal blocks you and you genuinely need the answer, the
**SupportHandoff** card at the bottom of the chat will compose an email
to your tenant's nominated support inbox and include the redacted
transcript.

## 7. Audit, governance, and observability

Every state-changing action — approving a warning, issuing a task,
publishing a snapshot, switching persona — appends to the immutable
**audit trail** (`audit_entries`, see spec-036 R-11 / spec-038 FR-020).
Audit rows are append-only and cryptographically chained per tenant; you
cannot edit or delete a historical entry from the UI.

To inspect the audit trail:
1. Open `/governance/audit` (Observer/Auditor lands here automatically).
2. Filter by actor, action type, surface, or correlation ID.
3. Export an evidence pack via `/governance/evidence` (Executive Leader
   and Observer/Auditor only) — emits a JSON + signed manifest archive
   suitable for post-incident review or AAO scrutiny.

The **Governance Dashboard** (`/governance/dashboard`) surfaces the ten
constitutional gates in real time. A red gate halts state-changing
operations until the constitutional steward acknowledges the breach.

## 8. Offline / degraded mode

The Hub assumes connectivity but degrades gracefully:
- **Field Coordinator** workspace and every **Sector** page register a
  service worker that queues critical actions (signal capture, status
  updates, evacuation acknowledgements).
- The action queue persists across browser restarts in IndexedDB. When
  connectivity returns, the queue replays in capture order and each
  action records both the **observed time** and the **synced time** in
  the audit trail.
- A persistent banner reads *"Offline — N actions queued"*. Click it to
  inspect or, with extreme caution, drop a queued action (audit-logged).
- Map tiles are aggressively cached for AU subdivisions you have visited
  in the prior thirty days. Out-of-cache regions render as a low-fidelity
  raster with a "Stale tile · last refresh DD/MM" stamp.

## 9. Accessibility

Every Hub screen meets WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-only navigation is fully
supported; the help affordance opens with `Ctrl+/`, the persona switcher
with `Alt+P`, the jurisdiction filter with `Alt+J`. See
[`accessibility-how-to`](/help/articles/accessibility-how-to) for the
complete shortcut map and screen-reader notes.

## 10. Where to next

- New persona? Open *your* persona's landing article from the persona
  switcher's "Help on this role" link.
- Faced with a specific screen? Search `/help/search` or press `Ctrl+/`
  with the screen open — the assistant uses the current `surface_id` as
  a retrieval hint.
- Running an exercise? Start with [`playbooks/simulation`](/help/articles/playbooks/simulation).
- Got an audit question? Start with [`audit-observer-mode`](/help/articles/audit-observer-mode).
