# HYP-030 - PH capstone typhoon hypothesis for 009-evacuation-agent

## Hypothesis
We believe pre-warned typhoon evacuation routing across archipelagic terrain will produce recommended evacuation start >= 24h earlier than the unaided NDRRMC reference timeline for the Philippines operator persona; we will know we are wrong if mean lead-time uplift < 12h across exercise scenarios during PH typhoon season Jun-Nov is observed in the measurement window (PH typhoon season Jun-Nov).

## Preconditions

- NDRRMC tabletop scenario pack available.
- D-009 cleared.

## Measurement Window
PH typhoon season Jun-Nov.

## Falsification Criterion
- mean lead-time uplift < 12h across exercise scenarios during PH typhoon season Jun-Nov.
