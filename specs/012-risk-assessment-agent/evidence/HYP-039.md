# HYP-039 - PH capstone typhoon hypothesis for 012-risk-assessment-agent

## Hypothesis
We believe typhoon (Pacific basin) risk-index calibration using PAGASA + historical track data will produce risk-index calibration error <= 15% on out-of-sample historical typhoon events for the Philippines operator persona; we will know we are wrong if calibration error > 25% on out-of-sample PH events during PH typhoon season Jun-Nov is observed in the measurement window (PH typhoon season Jun-Nov).

## Preconditions
- Out-of-sample PH typhoon corpus prepared (>=10 historical events).
- PH hazard-class taxonomy ratified by `risk-manager` actor.
- D-009 cleared.

## Measurement Window
PH typhoon season Jun-Nov.

## Falsification Criterion
- calibration error > 25% on out-of-sample PH events during PH typhoon season Jun-Nov.

Not tested
