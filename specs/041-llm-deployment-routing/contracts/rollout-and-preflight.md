### Separate collection and activation authority (I1)

G0 is **scoped evidence-collection authorisation**, recorded before any Azure
read or validation: approver, target tenant/subscription/account/environment,
allowed read operations, credentials, window and validation scope. It may
authorise inventory/governance reads needed for G1/G2 without already-approved
baseline evidence. After G1–G3 and local candidate review, a separately recorded
G0 validation scope may permit target metadata reads, CI/CD What-If and strict
carve-out validation needed to assemble G4 evidence. What-If remains mandatory;
do not replace it with offline fixtures or assume that read access permits it.
G0 never permits canary inference, activation, switching, promotion,
provisioning or deletion. No G0 authority is supplied by this document.

G4 is the later P1 release decision based on collected passing evidence,
including reviewed What-If. Record which canary/switch/promotion stages are
authorised; approval for one stage is not approval for another. Missing
collection authority blocks collection; missing release authority blocks
activation, not previously authorised evidence collection. G5/G6 remain
independent for operational migration/retirement.

### Separate migration provisioning and traffic authority (I4)

G5 comprises two approvals. **G5a** authorises replacement provisioning
against an approved target-bound proposal, baseline, governance, reviewed
What-If, actual-pool coexistence headroom and migration window. Model and
protection parity at this point concern the proposed configuration; actual
replacement readiness is not a prerequisite because replacements do not yet
exist. G5a grants no inference, traffic-switch or retirement authority.

After provisioning, G0-authorised metadata reads verify successful provisioning,
actual model/version/SKU/capacity/effective-policy/access parity and readiness.
These evidence checks do not themselves permit inference or switching.
**G5b** then requires that actual evidence, compatible candidate revisions,
the retained rollback pair and separately recorded approval for the permitted
canary, switch and forward/rollback stages, with their window and rollback
scope. Provisioning approval alone cannot satisfy G5b. Missing post-provision
evidence blocks traffic-stage approval, not otherwise approved provisioning.
G6 remains the independent retirement decision.

## Baseline/preflight input and failure rules

For every target environment, authorised read-only collection must establish:

1. Subscription, resource group, account ID, actual endpoint/region and
   environment scope; prevent cross-environment comparisons.
2. Approved existing deployment names and each deployment's model.name,
   model.version, format, SKU, capacity, RAI policy attachment/effective
   content filters and version-upgrade setting.
3. Current RBAC/managed identity, network/authentication and diagnostic/residency
   controls, including embedding. Preserve all regulated data persistence in AU.
4. Current image digest/revision and environment snapshot for API and agents.
5. Timestamped approval and reconciliation of repository proposal, approved
   baseline and actual Azure properties.

Compare configured/output per-tier model metadata against live deployment
properties and ensure each explicit name exists under the intended account.
A missing deployment, unreadable property, absent approval, scope mismatch or
metadata/protection discrepancy fails preflight. Do not substitute alias text,
repository defaults, another environment or supplied dev observations.
Repeat verification after switching to detect drift; rollback on mismatch.

### Approved freshness and invalidation policy (U3)

Every evidence bundle must name an approved policy/version and approver. The
policy defines permitted observation-to-use windows (if any), explicit
invalidation events and revalidation requirements for baseline, governance,
AVM compatibility, metadata, What-If and rollback evidence. No universal TTL is
implied or invented. Bind each item to target tenant/subscription/resource
group/account/environment, collection timestamp, source revision and applicable
candidate configuration digest, API/agents image revisions and compiled
IaC/parameter revision. A pre-candidate baseline may be reused only through a
recorded reconciliation to the exact candidate under that policy.

Changed destination/model/protection/embedding settings, policy/RBAC/network
state, candidate image/config or compiled proposal revisions, scope changes,
expired approvals/windows or detected drift invalidate dependent evidence.
The approved policy identifies which items must be recollected/reapproved;
reuse is never inferred from an old `approved` state or a recent timestamp.
Absent/unapproved policy, missing bindings, stale evidence, or unevaluated
invalidation means **STOP** for target readiness/activation and affected IaC
gates, not silent acceptance. Offline fixtures can test this failure without
selecting a production policy. Refresh requires the applicable G0 authority;
refresh itself does not grant G4/G5 approval.

**Initial-rollout diff invariant**: zero deployment renames, creates,
replacements, deletions, model/version changes, SKU/capacity changes,
RAI/filter changes or access/residency changes. Configuration/application
revision changes are intentional and must be reviewed separately.

## P1 rollout: existing resource identities only

1. **Approve/reconcile** each target baseline and governance/AVM evidence
   before future IaC changes. No generic dev-to-prod promotion of model values.
2. **Prepare compatible readers** for optional provenance fields. Validate
   historical rows and absence semantics before enabling new producers.
3. **Prepare configuration producer**: explicit old deployment names,
   independent approved model/version metadata, collision-free tier CSV,
   retained embedding and unambiguous legacy keys. Test compiled outputs
   against the target baseline; reject unexpected resource differences.
4. **Build matched candidates**: pair corrected application image and cleaned
   environment snapshot for API and agents. Preflight the exact raw CSV and
   identity metadata before candidate activation. There is no strict-reader
   release against the old conflicting CSV and no permanent permissive flag.
5. **Verify before switch**: hermetic common/MAF/controller/dispatcher checks,
   every role, all shared-model orderings, SSE/tools/citations, audit failure
   paths, embedding and supported eval-map behaviour. For future authorised
   canary inference, use synthetic approved data and approved capacity only.
6. **Switch paired revisions** with minimal exposure, run post-switch identity,
   route, streaming, degradation and audit checks. Scale/promote only if all
   gates pass; otherwise leave the previous active revision unchanged.
7. **Rollback** restores a verified compatible image/configuration pair, not
   only an env var or CSV. Never replay the old ambiguous map into the strict
   image. Prefer the last verified corrected pair. If the only pre-P1 pair
   has the known simulation misrouting defect, it is not a safe simulation
   rollback target: keep simulation inference unavailable through existing
   operational safety controls until a corrected candidate passes. Do not
   route simulation to live capacity to maintain availability.

No deployment resource is renamed or replaced during these steps. An
environment-only update must not be blindly pushed to an old dispatcher:
the old shared-model-key path is not compatible with omitted ambiguous keys.
Candidate code/configuration are the activation unit.

## Completion and acceptance levels (I3)

1. **Local implementation/procedure verification**: hermetic routing/audit/
   preflight tests and synthetic P2 tabletop demonstrate implementation and
   procedure coverage only; external gates may remain blocked.
2. **Target P1 release readiness**: current target-bound evidence under the
   approved freshness policy, G1–G3, compatible readers and matched candidates,
   mandatory reviewed What-If/carve-out/metadata checks and G4 stage approvals.
   Readiness is not proof of rollout execution or operational authority.
3. **Real P2 operational migration acceptance**: independently authorised G5
   target execution with actual replacement readiness, traffic switch,
   post-switch verification and rollback verification retaining chat/streaming
   behaviour and unchanged models. This supplies SC-006 operational evidence;
   synthetic rehearsal does not. Retention follows approved criteria; actual
   retirement still requires G6. P1 does not require opting into P2.

## P2 separately opt-in purpose-name migration

1. **Approve a migration proposal** independently of P1; record old/new names,
   unchanged model/version/protections, approver, window, rollback pair and
   verification/retention criteria.
2. **Prove coexistence headroom** in the actual subscription/region/model/SKU
   quota pool. Include old plus new allocations and operational margin.
   A separate deployment isolates allocations; it does not create quota.
   No unapproved capacity increases or model substitution.
3. **Check proposed policies and access** for each replacement against the
   approved effective RAI policy and access posture without unapproved
   changes; verify permitted region/model, reviewed What-If and operation
   ordering. Record G5a provisioning approval after these proposal checks and
   coexistence headroom pass; do not require readiness of uncreated resources.
4. **Provision replacements only after G5a approval**, using reviewed IaC/CI-CD.
   Old deployments remain. Under G0 metadata-read authority, verify
   provisioning success and actual model, version, SKU, capacity,
   effective-policy/access parity and readiness before G5b traffic approval.
5. **Create candidate revisions** pointing each tier to its replacement.
   Retain the complete old corrected image/configuration pair and old
   resources. Check candidate compatibility for common, MAF, controller and
   all dispatcher roles using local evidence; creating inactive candidates
   does not authorise inference or activation.
6. **Obtain G5b traffic-stage approval, then switch and verify** paired
   API/agents revisions. Record the permitted canary/switch/forward/rollback
   stages, window and rollback scope against actual replacement readiness and
   candidate evidence. Verify truthful provenance, streaming and isolation
   and capture route/audit evidence within that scope. On any failed forward
   check, use the approved rollback scope to restore the old corrected pair,
   reverify it, and retain both sets for investigation.
7. **Retain old resources** through the explicitly approved observation period
   and successful rollback rehearsal. Do not infer duration or retirement
   approval from test success.
8. **Retire only by separate approval**, after proving no active revision,
   worker, evaluation or rollback target depends on the old identities.
   Update operational references and evidence. No automatic deletion is part
   of application startup, Bicep cleanup or this feature's initial rollout.
