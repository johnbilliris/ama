## Notes

- Checked: 37; unchecked blockers: 0; total: 37.
- Local-synthetic is the default ordinary Windows/Linux implementation path and requires no Azure VM or resource. Its owner-only mode-bound daemon uses peer-authenticated IPC, synthetic fixtures/fake transports, and an ephemeral two-phase journal with `execution_mode=local-synthetic` and `audit_sink=ephemeral-local`; it creates no repository or qualifying evidence and makes no sovereignty, release, SC-002, read-only-evidence, or external-success claim.
- Sovereign signed preflight with audience `spec001-extension-list-sovereign-preflight`, AU VM/IMDS/encryption, managed identity/private networking, immutable ledger, and authorized offline AU cache remains required only for normal non-synthetic use, qualifying read-only/SC-002 evidence, CI release, and real collect/finalize. Sovereign VM execution/AU-store review and the 20-person study remain pending external gates; pending status is neither local failure nor completion evidence.
- Local custody loss may start a new explicitly discontinuous non-evidentiary segment; sovereign custody loss after product edits remains terminal. T008 starts local-synthetic and captures the local baseline, T009 is the first product edit, and the action clause is Post-T008.
