# HYP-044 - Spec-006 Actor-Consultation Uplift Evidence Placeholder

## Hypothesis

We believe that requiring actor consultation for each spec-006 change will produce at least a +1 rubric-band improvement for the Assumptions, Requirements, Personas, Hypotheses, and Scope criteria relative to the recorded baseline; we will know we are wrong if any one of those criteria regresses or the mean uplift is below +1 in the same review window.

## Preconditions

- A per-spec-006 baseline and post-change rubric comparator are recorded with immutable revision references.
- Actor-consultation evidence for each change is captured with reviewer identity, method, and timestamp.

## Measurement Window

From first approved spec-006 amendment after this stub through the next explicit rubric reassessment cycle.

## Falsification Criterion

- Mean uplift across the five criteria is less than +1 compared to the baseline.
- Any of the five criteria scores lower than its baseline value.
