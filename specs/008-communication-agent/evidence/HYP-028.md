# HYP-028 - JP capstone subduction-zone earthquake / tsunami hypothesis for 008-communication-agent

## Hypothesis
We believe a Japanese language pack with JMA-aligned warning-tier templates and content-safety classifier will produce true-positive misinformation detection >= 85% on known JP samples and earthquake-alert delivery latency p95 <= 5s for the Japan operator persona; we will know we are wrong if true-positive rate < 70% on the labelled ja-JP validation corpus or earthquake-alert latency p95 > 10s during quarterly review is observed in the measurement window (JP continuous; quarterly review).

## Preconditions
- JMA / Cabinet Office DRR data-licensing agreement signed.
- ja-JP training corpus reviewed by >=1 native-speaker content-safety reviewer.
- D-009 capstone-reinterpretation decision cleared.

## Measurement Window
JP continuous; quarterly review.

## Falsification Criterion
- true-positive rate < 70% on the labelled ja-JP validation corpus or earthquake-alert latency p95 > 10s during quarterly review.

## Status
Not tested
