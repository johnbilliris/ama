## Page 1

DPFEM 
Future Focus 2025-28
Our Vision
A safe, secure and resilient Tasmania.
Our Mission
To provide effective police, fire and emergency management services.
Our Environment
DPFEM is continuing to operate within a challenging and uncertain environment. 
•	 We are responding to increasing community expectations at a time of significant fiscal constraint and legislative change. 
•	 We are adapting to workforce changes to continue to recruit and retain at the level that is needed to keep the community safe. 
•	 We are responding to changing environmental conditions which impact our emergency management responses.
Strategic Focus Areas
People
DPFEM has a dedicated workforce 
who are committed to supporting and 
protecting our community. 
DPFEM will support our people by:
•	 Supporting the physical and mental 
wellbeing of our people.
•	 Prioritising actions to keep them safe.
•	 Building a positive and 
collaborative culture. 
•	 Enhancing the capability and leadership 
of our people.
Community
Keeping our community safe is the primary 
objective of DPFEM. 
DPFEM will keep our community safe by: 
•	 Delivering a reliable and highly 
visible response. 
•	 Proactive and inclusive engagement with 
the community.
•	 Helping our community to build their 
preparedness and resilience. 
Business
Effective business practices are critical to 
the operation of DPFEM. 
DPFEM will enhance our business by: 
•	 Prioritising good governance 
and accountability. 
•	 Fostering a culture of continuous learning 
and improvement.  
•	 Proactively assessing our risks and 
making evidence-based decisions. 
DPFEM will have a collective focus on these three areas through: 
Tasmania Police Strategy
TFS and SES Strategic Plan 
BES Action Plan
