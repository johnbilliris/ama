South Australian Emergency Services Sector
Sector structure
•	 The Emergency Services 
Sector (ESS) consists of three 
operational public facing 
Emergency Service Organisations 
(CFS, MFS and SES) and a 
sector-wide support agency 
(SAFECOM) 
•	
Funding for the ESS is provided 
from the CESF with some 
additional funding coming through 
grants and supplementary income 
being generated by ESO’s. 
Funding is constrained in that 
the ESL is the sole government 
source of funding with the levy 
rate being determined by the 
Treasurer as is the allocation of 
funding to each ESO
•	 SAFECOM will develop and 
maintain a strategic and policy 
framework across the ESS
Sector core 
principles
•	 Commitment to achieving sector 
harmonisation, alignment and 
modernisation to maximise public value 
in terms of effectiveness and efficiency 
in meeting community expectations 
•	 Strategic planning that incorporates the 
National Principles and Five priorities 
articulated in the “Strategic Directions” 
document
•	 Adoption (where possible) of agreed 
standards to maintain national 
interoperability
•	 Community safety models based 
on the principles of Prevention, 
Preparedness, Response and 
Recovery (PPRR) to deliver a 
comprehensive approach to risk 
management
•	 Ongoing commitment to building 
community resilience
•	 Increased emphasis on Prevention and 
Preparedness
Sector roles
•	 To support the vision of “we are safe 
in our homes, community and at 
work” contained within the South 
Australian Strategic Plan 
•	 To protect life, property and 
environmental assets while ensuring 
the safety, health and wellbeing of 
our workforce 
•	 To meet the statutory obligations 
and responsibilities under the FES 
Act 2005, EM Act 2004 and other 
applicable Acts and regulations 
•	 To fulfil obligations as a Hazard 
Leader, Control Agency or Support 
Agency defined within the State 
Emergency Management Plan
•	 Each ESS agency (incl SAFECOM) 
will develop, maintain and implement 
agency specific strategic and 
business plans
•	 SAFECOM will provide functional 
support to each ESO
•	 Evidence based strategic 
decisionmaking and prioritisation of 
better community outcomes. Evidence 
will include use of data, community 
consultation, decision-making tools, 
inquiry recommendations and other 
evaluations
•	 “Change ready” and keen to embrace 
continuous improvement
•	 Recognition that our strength is our 
people. Our plans place appropriate 
emphasis on the wellbeing of our 
staff and volunteers, their career 
development, their recognition and the 
observance of high ethical standards
